Howrah Bridge

Overview: An iconic balanced cantilever bridge over the Hooghly River, connecting Kolkata and Howrah.

History & Significance: Commissioned in 1943 during World War II without any nuts or bolts, it was built using riveted high-tensile steel. It remains one of the busiest cantilever bridges in the world and a defining symbol of Kolkata.
