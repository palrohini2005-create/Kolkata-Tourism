Writer's Building

Overview: The historic secretariat building of the state government of West Bengal.

History & Significance: Designed by Thomas Lyon in 1777 for junior writers of the East India Company, it features a red-brick Greco-Roman facade. Expanded over time, it became a prominent centre of administration and a landmark around B.B.D. Bagh.
