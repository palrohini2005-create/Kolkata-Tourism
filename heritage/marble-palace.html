Marble Palace

Overview: A palatial 19th-century mansion in North Kolkata known for its marble walls, floors, and sculptures.

History & Significance: Built in 1835 by Raja Rajendra Mullick, this neoclassical mansion houses European artwork and antique furniture. Its richly decorated interiors offer a glimpse into the collecting and domestic life of a prominent 19th-century Bengali family.
