Indian Museum

Overview: The ninth oldest museum in the world and the largest museum in India.

History & Significance: Established in 1814 by the Asiatic Society of Bengal, the museum has rare collections of antiques, armor, ornaments, fossils, mummies, and Mughal paintings. Its galleries document natural history, art, archaeology, and the many cultures of the region.
