Prinsep Ghat

Overview: A riverside ghat along the Kolkata bank of the Hooghly, featuring Greek and Gothic architectural styles.

History & Significance: Constructed in 1841 in memory of Anglo-Indian scholar James Prinsep, the white Palladian porch is one of Kolkata's best-known riverfront landmarks. It offers views of the Hooghly and the nearby Vidyasagar Setu.
