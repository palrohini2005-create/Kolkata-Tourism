Kalighat Kali Temple

Overview: One of the 51 Shakti Peethas, dedicated to Goddess Kali.

History & Significance: The present temple structure was completed in 1809 under the patronage of the Sabarna Roy Choudhury family, though worship at the site dates back centuries earlier. The temple remains one of Bengal's most important pilgrimage destinations.
